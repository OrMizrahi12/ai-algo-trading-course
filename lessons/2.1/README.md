# Student kit: Lesson 2.1, Your broker: IBKR accounts and installing TWS

Module 2: Markets, live in TWS · AI Algorithmic Trading with Claude Code and Interactive Brokers

In this lesson you saw which Interactive Brokers (IBKR) account this course needs (a funded and opened IBKR Pro
account, with the paper account that comes with it), what a paper account is, which market data you get for free,
and how market data reaches the paper account. Then you watched Trader Workstation (TWS) being downloaded (the
Stable version), installed on Linux, and logged in to a paper account, where you saw what the free data looks like:
US stock quotes, delayed, and no forex. This course trades US stocks and ETFs on that free data.

## What's in this kit

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `account-checklist.md` | The account you need: IBKR Pro, funded and opened, the paper account, two-factor login |
| `install-tws.md` | Exact steps to download, install and first-launch TWS on Windows, macOS and Linux |
| `market-data-and-permissions.md` | Market data status and the API acknowledgement; optional extras this course does not need (real-time data, sharing it with paper, forex, prices) |

## Try it yourself

1. Check your account against `account-checklist.md`.
2. Install TWS Stable for your system with `install-tws.md` and open it on the **Paper Trading** tab.
3. Do sections 1 and 2 of `market-data-and-permissions.md` in your account settings, then log out of TWS and log
   back in. Nothing in this course needs a paid subscription.
4. Log in to paper and look at your watchlist. Delayed prices sit on a dark olive background: that is the free data
   this course works with. (Real-time prices, an optional paid add-on, have a plain dark background.)

## Remember

- Everything in this course runs on a **paper (simulated) account**. Never log in to the Live Trading tab for
  anything in this course.
- Your paper account number and usernames are private. Don't post them in screenshots or forums.
- Prices, menus and version numbers change. What you see on IBKR's pages today wins over this kit.
- This is educational content, not financial advice.

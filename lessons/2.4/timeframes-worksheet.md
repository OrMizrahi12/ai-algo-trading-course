# Five timeframes of one chart

Lesson 2.4 · TWS, Mosaic layout, logged in to the **paper** account, free delayed data. Menus can move between
versions. Lesson 2.5 covers the chart tools in depth; here you only change the bar size.

## The steps in TWS

1. Select `SPY` in a watchlist. Its chart follows (or open a chart from the symbol's right-click menu).
2. Optional: click the chart panel's maximize button so every candle has room.
3. Click the chart's bar-size menu (the title, for example "Today/5 min candles"). The menu has two parts: **Candle
   Size** at the top and **Time Period** below. Each time period opens a list of bar sizes.
4. Pick, one after the other:
   - **Today** > **1 min**: one candle per minute; a full session (9:30 to 16:00 New York time) is 390 candles.
   - **Today** > **5 min**: one candle per five minutes; a full session is 78 candles.
   - **1 Month** > **Hourly**: about seven candles per day.
   - **1 Year** > **Daily**: one candle per trading day; today's is the last one, still forming.
   - **1 Year** > **Weekly**: one candle per week; this week is the last one.
5. Hover a candle. The line under the chart shows its time, high (H), low (L) and close (C).
6. Click the maximize button again to restore the layout.

With free delayed data, the newest candle is about 15 minutes behind the market.

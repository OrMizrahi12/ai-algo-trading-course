# Student kit: Lesson 2.4, Trading styles on real charts

Module 2: Markets from zero, on a real trading platform · AI Algorithmic Trading with Claude Code and Interactive Brokers

In this lesson you put six trading styles on one line by how long they hold a trade, from high-frequency trading
(microseconds) through scalping, day trading, swing trading and position trading to investing (years), and compare
them on holding time, capital, your time, stress and costs. Then you read one instrument, SPY, in Trader Workstation
(TWS) on the paper account, on five timeframes: 1-minute, 5-minute, hourly, daily and weekly candles. Finally, the
reasons this course day trades on 5-minute bars.

## What's in this kit

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `styles-sheet.md` | The six styles compared on one page: holding time, capital, your time, stress, costs, overnight risk |
| `timeframes-worksheet.md` | The lesson's chart steps in TWS, click by click, and a worksheet to fill in on your own paper account |

## Try it yourself

1. In TWS, logged in to your **paper** account, open a chart of `SPY`.
2. In the chart's bar-size menu, flip through **Today / 1 min**, **Today / 5 min**, **1 Month / Hourly**,
   **1 Year / Daily** and **1 Year / Weekly**.
3. On each chart, hover one candle and write down its high, its low and how far apart they are
   (`timeframes-worksheet.md`).
4. Write down how many hours a day you could really watch a screen, and which styles fit that.
5. You're done when you can name the six styles in order of holding time, and say why day traders avoid overnight
   risk.

## Remember

- Everything in this course runs on a **paper (simulated) account**.
- Your paper account number is private. Don't post it in screenshots or forums.
- Prices change every day: your candles will show other values than the lesson's. What your own TWS shows wins.
- This is educational content, not financial advice. No style is "best", and none is a promise of profit; this course
  picks day trading on 5-minute bars for teaching reasons.

# Student kit: Lesson 2.5, TWS as your charting platform

Module 2: Markets from zero, on a real trading platform · AI Algorithmic Trading with Claude Code and Interactive Brokers

In this lesson you find your way around Trader Workstation (TWS) in its Mosaic layout, build a watchlist called
"Day trading" with SPY, QQQ, AAPL, MSFT and NVDA, add Bid and Ask columns and read a quote. Then you set up a chart of
SPY on 5-minute candles over two days, add two indicators (an EMA of 20 and VWAP) plus volume, draw a horizontal line
and a trend line, link the chart to the watchlist, and save the whole workspace as a layout.

## What's in this kit

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `menu-paths-cheat-sheet.md` | Every click of the lesson, menu by menu, exactly as TWS showed it, plus a checklist for your own workspace |

## Try it yourself

1. Log in to TWS with your **paper** account (Mosaic layout).
2. Build a watchlist with SPY and a few US stocks you know, with **Bid** and **Ask** columns.
3. Set a chart to **2 Days / 5 min candles**, add an **EMA** with period 20, **Intraday VWAP** and **Volume**.
4. Draw one horizontal line and one trend line (press, drag, release).
5. Copy the Mosaic tab to a new layout, then **File > Layout Settings > Save Settings**.
6. You're done when clicking a stock in your watchlist switches the chart (studies and all), and your layout shows up
   under **File > Open Layout Library > My Layouts**.

## Remember

- Everything in this course runs on a **paper (simulated) account**.
- Your paper account number is private (TWS shows it at the top right and in some dialog titles). Don't post it in
  screenshots or forums.
- Free market data is **delayed**, about 15 minutes behind the market. TWS shows delayed cells with an olive
  background. Fine for learning; this course never pays for data.
- TWS menus can move between versions. If a menu looks different, the cheat sheet tells you what to look for.
- Prices change every day: your quotes and candles will show other values than the lesson's.
- This is educational content, not financial advice. The symbols in the lesson are examples, not recommendations.

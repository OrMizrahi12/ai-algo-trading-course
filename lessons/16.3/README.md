# Student kit: Lesson 16.3, Before real money

Module 16: Capstone · AI Algorithmic Trading with Claude Code and Interactive Brokers

Everything in this course ran on a paper account, with simulated money. This closing lesson is not a signal to go live.
It covers what changes between paper and a live account, and which rules apply, read on the official pages
themselves: Interactive Brokers on paper fills, FINRA's 2026 notice on the day-trading margin rules, and Anthropic's
Usage Policy and Consumer Terms. You leave with an educational checklist.

Educational content only. This is not financial, legal or tax advice, and nothing here is a promise of profit or a
recommendation to trade real money. Deciding not to trade real money is a perfectly good outcome of this course.

## What's in this kit

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `checklist.md` | The educational checklist from the lesson, as questions to answer in your own words: education, risk capital, size, logs, kill switch, review gate, the rules you've read |
| `paper-vs-live.md` | A cheat sheet: what changes from paper to live (fills, data, costs, you), the three key terms, and what each official page says |
| `reading-list.md` | The official pages from the lesson, with their dates as shown on screen, plus the pages to look up yourself (your broker's, your country's) |

## Try it yourself

1. Open the four pages in `reading-list.md` and read them in their current versions. Terms and policies change: the
   versions in the video are the ones that were current in October 2026.
2. Write down which terms cover the way you use Claude: the Consumer Terms (they govern Claude.ai, Claude Pro
   and other services for individuals) or the Commercial Terms (they govern any Anthropic API key).
3. Look up your own country's rules on trading and on taxes, and your broker's terms for live accounts.
4. Fill in every line of `checklist.md`.

You're done when every line of the checklist has an answer, even if the answer is no.

## Good to know

- **Read the source, not a summary.** The lesson reads the pages as they are and adds no interpretation. If a rule
  matters to you, read the whole page, and ask a qualified professional where you need one.
- **US rules are US rules.** FINRA's notice applies to US broker-dealers and their customers. Other countries have
  their own regulators and rules.
- **Paper only in this course.** Every order in the course went to a paper account. Nothing here tells you to move to a
  live account, or when.

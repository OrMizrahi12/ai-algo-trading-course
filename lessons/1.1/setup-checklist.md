# Setup checklist (Lesson 1.1)

Answer each line before Module 2. Nothing here needs to be bought today: the lessons show every step.

## 1. A computer

- [ ] Windows, macOS or Linux. The course is filmed on Linux; every install lesson's kit has exact steps for the
      other two systems.
- [ ] Room to install a few programs (Trader Workstation, IB Gateway, VS Code, Claude Code, Obsidian) and a few
      gigabytes of market data.
- [ ] An internet connection. For the live-on-paper lessons, a computer that stays awake while the market is open
      (a laptop that sleeps is not a server).

## 2. A Claude plan (Lesson 3.2)

- [ ] Claude Pro or Claude Max. Pro is enough to start; Max earns its price in part 2, when Claude makes a decision
      on every bar.
- [ ] No Anthropic API key. Everything runs on the subscription. If an `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is set on your computer,
      Lesson 3.2 shows why to remove it.

## 3. An Interactive Brokers account, used on paper (Lessons 2.1 and 4.1)

- [ ] An IBKR Pro account (API access needs a funded and opened IBKR Pro account; the free trial is not that account).
- [ ] Its paper account and paper username. Every order in this course goes to the paper account. Never a live one.
- [ ] Market data: the free data is enough (price history and 15-minute delayed quotes). Lesson 2.1 covers the
      non-professional status and the market data API acknowledgement.

## 4. Free accounts and tools you'll meet later

- [ ] A GitHub account (Lesson 3.3), for a private copy of your Trading Lab.
- [ ] Obsidian (Lesson 13.1): free, no account needed.

## Rules we follow all course

- [ ] Paper account only. Educational content, not financial advice.
- [ ] Read your AI provider's terms and your broker's terms (Lessons 1.2 and 16.3).
- [ ] Any real-money decision is yours alone.

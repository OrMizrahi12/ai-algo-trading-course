# Check target is twice the stop distance before buying

Before buying, check that the target is at least twice as far from entry as the stop, and if it is not, pick a closer stop level or skip the trade.

Taught by [[2026-10-21 1245 SPY]], seen once: one trade is an anecdote, not a pattern. Written by the desk's reviewer as a proposal; only a person makes a lesson active, and the constitution and the risk rules win over any lesson.

Words used: **bar** = open, high, low, close and volume of one 5-minute slice of SPY. **R** = 1% of the account's value, the money lost if the stop is hit. **stop** = a resting order that sells at your loss limit. **target** = a resting order that sells at your profit goal.

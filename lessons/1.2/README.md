# Student kit: Lesson 1.2, Three ways to trade, and where AI fits

Module 1: Welcome · AI Algorithmic Trading with Claude Code and Interactive Brokers

In this lesson you saw three ways to trade side by side: a person reading a chart (discretionary trading), rules a
computer follows (systematic, or algorithmic, trading), and an AI that makes the decision itself (an AI agent). You
also saw how the two halves of this course map onto the last two ways, and the rules of the road we follow.

## What's in this kit

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `three-ways-comparison.md` | One page: the three ways to trade, with the strengths and the limits of each |
| `prompts.md` | The exact prompts typed in the lesson, so you can try them yourself in Claude |
| `idea-to-rules-worksheet.md` | The "pause and try" worksheet: turn one trading idea into exact rules |
| `rules-of-the-road.md` | The checklist we follow for the whole course (paper account, terms, your responsibility) |

## Try it yourself

1. Get some price bars. Any free source of 5-minute bars works (many charting sites and data providers let you
   export a CSV with time, open, high, low and close). From module 5 on, we pull data straight from Interactive
   Brokers. The data file used on screen is not included in this kit.
2. Open Claude (claude.ai or the Claude desktop app), start a new chat and attach your CSV file.
3. Paste the prompts from `prompts.md`, one at a time, as in the lesson.
4. Compare what you get with what you saw. Your results will be different: different data, a different day, and
   (for the AI-trader prompt) a fresh answer every time. That's expected.

## Remember

- Everything in this course runs on a **paper (simulated) account**. Nothing here is financial advice.
- A good backtest is evidence, not proof. Five trades prove nothing either way.
- Read your AI provider's terms and your broker's terms before you use AI for anything to do with trading.

# Rules of the road: the checklist for this course

Read it once now, and again before you ever consider trading real money.

## In this course

- [ ] **Paper account only.** Every order in this course goes to an Interactive Brokers paper (simulated) account:
      real prices, simulated money, nothing real at risk.
- [ ] **Education, not financial advice.** The course teaches methods and tools. Nothing in it is a recommendation to
      buy or sell anything, and no strategy in it is presented as profitable.
- [ ] **No holy grail.** A good backtest is evidence, not proof. A few trades, or a few days of data, prove nothing
      either way. Your results will differ from ours, and that's expected.

## Terms you should read yourself

- [ ] **Your AI provider's terms.** For example, Anthropic's Consumer Terms (anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms,
      the version effective Oct 8, 2025, as shown in the lesson) list, among the ways you may not use the services
      (section 3, item 9), relying on them to buy or sell securities or to provide or receive advice about securities
      and other financial products, and say (section 11) that your use is solely at your own risk. Terms change:
      read the current version.
- [ ] **Your broker's terms** for automated and API trading, and the rules where you live.

## Before real money (if you ever decide to)

- [ ] It is your decision and your responsibility.
- [ ] You understand every rule your system follows, and every limit that stops it.
- [ ] You have tested on paper for long enough to judge the results, with realistic costs.
- [ ] You only risk money you can afford to lose.
- [ ] You know how to stop everything at once (a kill switch), and you have tried it.

Lesson 16.3 "Before real money" goes through this list in depth.

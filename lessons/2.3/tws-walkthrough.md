# The lesson's steps in TWS

Lesson 2.3 · TWS (Stable 10.50), Mosaic layout, logged in to the **paper** account. Menus can move between versions.
Nothing here buys, enables or subscribes to anything.

## 1. The instrument chooser

1. In the Favorites watchlist (Monitor panel), click an empty row.
2. Type a symbol and press **Enter**. TWS lists every product with that name, grouped by company and venue.
3. Pick the plain **Stock** line of the company you mean. The row is added, and the linked panels (order ticket,
   quote, chart) follow the new symbol.

Try: `AMD` (a stock: options, warrants, CFDs, other listings, corporate bonds), `DIA` (an ETF: its type is Stock),
`MES` (pick **Futures**, then a month in the expiry list; note "1 pt = $5.00"), `BTC` (funds, companies, CME bitcoin
futures; the cryptocurrency lines are greyed out on our account; press **Esc** to close).

## 2. An option chain

1. Select `SPY` in the watchlist.
2. In the order ticket, click **Option Chain**. The expiry dates are tabs across the top, the strikes run down the
   middle, calls on the left, puts on the right. The prices are delayed and take a few seconds to fill in.
3. Close the window when you're done.

## 3. Contract specs

1. Right-click a stock's row, type `descr` in the menu's search box, and open **Financial Instrument Info >
   Description**.
2. Read: security type, currency, exchange and primary exchange, market data, trading schedule (regular session and
   total available hours; the times in brackets are your computer's clock), margin, shortable.
3. Do the same for a futures row: contract month, expiration date, multiplier, settlement method, minimum tick,
   trading hours, margin per contract.

## 4. Extended hours on a chart

1. Select `SPY`; in its chart, open the bar-size menu and pick **Today**, **5 min**.
2. Open **Edit > Chart Parameters**. Under **Chart Time Options**, tick **Show data outside of regular trading hours**
   and click **OK**.
3. The chart now starts at 04:00 (pre-market) and its time axis runs to 20:00 (after-hours). The setting changes only
   what the chart shows. Untick it to go back to the regular session.

# Cost worksheet

Lesson 2.6 · What one round trip really costs. Fill it in from the **Trades** tab (one line per fill, with price and
commission) and the **Portfolio** (Realized P&L).

## The lesson's round trip, worked out (SPY, 10 shares, paper account)

| Step | Lesson value |
|---|---|
| Bought 10 at | 767.58 |
| Sold 10 at | 769.07 |
| Price move per share (sell minus buy) | 1.49 |
| Gross result (move × shares) | 1.49 × 10 = **14.90** |
| Commission on the buy | 1.00 |
| Commission on the sell | 1.16 (a sale also pays small regulatory fees) |
| Net result (gross minus commissions) | 14.90 − 2.16 = **12.74** |
| What TWS showed as Realized | 12.7 |

The three costs of a trade:

1. **Commissions**: on every fill; you see them in the Trades tab.
2. **Spread**: buy at the ask, sell at the bid, and you pay the gap on every round trip. For SPY it's usually a cent or
   two a share.
3. **Slippage**: the gap between the price you expected and the fill you got. On a delayed screen with simulated paper
   fills you can't measure real slippage, but with real money it is real.

## Leverage arithmetic

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Your account | 10,000 |
| Margin used | 2 to 1 |
| Position | 20,000 |
| A 5% move in the stock | 5% × 20,000 = 1,000 |
| As a share of your account | 1,000 ÷ 10,000 = **10%**, up or down |

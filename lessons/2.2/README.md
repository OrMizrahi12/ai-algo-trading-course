# Student kit: Lesson 2.2, What a market is, live in TWS

Module 2: Markets, live in TWS · AI Algorithmic Trading with Claude Code and Interactive Brokers

In this lesson you met who is in a market (exchanges, your broker, market makers and everyone else) and followed one
order from your click to an exchange and back. Then, in Trader Workstation (TWS) on a paper account, you added the
columns a trader reads (Bid Size, Bid, Ask, Ask Size, Spread, Volume), read a real quote, compared a busy fund with a
quieter stock, and saw what free data shows of the order book: Time and Sales with a single "Delayed" row, and an
empty Market Depth window that offers paid feeds. Because the full book takes paid data, the lesson drew one, and
showed how an order eats the queue and moves the price.

## What's in this kit

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `quote-columns.md` | Add the quote columns to a TWS watchlist, step by step, and read each one |
| `spread-worksheet.md` | The "your turn" exercise: two symbols, each spread in cents and in percent, with the lesson's worked numbers |
| `order-book-explained.md` | The order book, a market order and a limit order, on the lesson's drawn example; what free data shows |

## Try it yourself

1. Log in to TWS on the **Paper Trading** tab.
2. Add the six columns with `quote-columns.md`.
3. Fill in `spread-worksheet.md` for one busy fund (like SPY) and one stock that trades much less.
4. Read `order-book-explained.md`, then explain to yourself, in one sentence, why a market order can move the price.

## Remember

- Everything in this course runs on a **paper (simulated) account**. Never log in to the Live Trading tab for
  anything in this course.
- Your paper account number is private: keep it out of screenshots and forum posts.
- The free data is delayed (about 15 minutes; olive background). This course never subscribes to paid data.
- Symbols in this lesson (SPY, IBIT, IBKR) are examples to read a quote on, not recommendations.
- Prices, menus and version numbers change. What you see in TWS today wins over this kit.
- This is educational content, not financial advice.
